United Nations Security Council

The UNSC will simulate the tense days of the Cuban Missile Crisis, frozen on 14 October 1962, when the United States discovered Soviet nuclear missiles stationed in Cuba. Delegates will step into the shoes of world leaders navigating one of the most dangerous confrontations of the Cold War.

United Nations Human Rights Council

The UNHRC will deliberate on the Rwandan Genocide of 1994, one of the most tragic failures of the international community to protect human rights. Delegates will examine the causes, consequences, and humanitarian response to mass atrocities.

International Press Corps

The IPC will assign delegates to cover proceedings in both the UNSC and UNHRC. Acting as journalists, correspondents, and media representatives, IPC members will report on debates, highlight key developments, and shape public perception.
